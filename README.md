# Krisztian Horvath

Research Assistant and PhD Student at the **Department of Whole Vehicle Engineering, Széchenyi István University, Győr, Hungary**. My research focuses on noise, vibration, and harshness (NVH) in geared electric drivetrains.

## Research focus

- Gear NVH and electric drivetrain vibroacoustics
- Gear microgeometry, pitch deviation, and tooth-surface waviness
- Transmission error and spectral order analysis
- Manufacturing and assembly variability
- Multibody dynamics and coupled vibroacoustic simulation
- Gear metrology and condition monitoring
- Data-driven and interpretable machine learning for NVH

## Repository roadmap

This profile is the identity and discovery layer for a developing research portfolio. Public repositories will be added only when they contain a substantive, citable research contribution rather than duplicated publisher or Zenodo files.

- **industrial-gearbox-nvh-data** — documentation and cross-links for publicly archived EOL, component rolling-test, and CMM research outputs
- **gear-microgeometry-nvh** — future public methods and examples for transmission error, pitch deviation, tooth-surface waviness, and spectral order analysis
- **reproducible-gearbox-nvh** — future reusable analysis and visualization workflows when release-cleared source code is available

## Reproducibility and data-access statement

Only materials that have been explicitly cleared for public release are linked or published here. Internal industrial measurements, CAD geometry, MBD/FEM models, source code, and other materials protected by partner agreements, NDA, or TISAX requirements are not distributed. Public repositories distinguish openly reproducible workflows from analyses that depend on restricted industrial inputs.
